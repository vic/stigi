Stigi stands on a lot of shoulders. Its ideas come from decades of research in types, logic, memory, effects and human factors, and from many projects that solved one piece of the puzzle well.

These pages list every project and paper we learned from, with a short note on what Stigi takes from each one. Links to papers point at the paper when we have a stable address, and at a scholar search otherwise.
